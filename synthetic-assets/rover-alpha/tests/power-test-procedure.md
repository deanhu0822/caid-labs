# T-PWR-24 — Power Test procedure

Test BAT-24-12, PDB-24V, MCTRL-8A, and the J12 43025-0400 path at idle, typical mission, curb start, and emergency stop. Verify energy estimate, fuse selectivity, BMS telemetry, J12 contact resistance, and undervoltage shutdown.

Acceptance: idle ≤1.8 A; typical power ≤95 W; peak battery current ≤36 A; J12 resistance ≤80 mΩ per power contact; shutdown 19.8–20.4 V.
