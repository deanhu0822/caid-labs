# T-CAM-12 — Camera Calibration procedure

Install CM-4K-R2 on CAM-MNT-C, load `camera.rs` profile CAL-CAM-12-C, capture 24 synthetic checkerboard poses at 3840×2160, and solve intrinsics/extrinsics. Acceptance: mean reprojection error ≤0.60 px, focal length 2500–2900 px, and mount roll within ±0.5°.
