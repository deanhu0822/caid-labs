# T-FINAL-C — Final QA procedure

Use QA-FIX-C after ROUTE-08. Validate identity/revision, J12 43025-0400 retention and pin telemetry, emergency stop, wheel direction, navigation readiness, camera stream, sealing inspection, and a 20 m autonomous route.

Acceptance includes: all serials Rev C compatible; J12 retention ≥30 N; enable response ≤20 ms; ADC3 current error ≤3%; autonomous route completed without safety stop.
