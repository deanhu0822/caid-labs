# WI-082 — Motor Installation, Rev C

## Configuration

Two MTR-24-220 motors, two MTR-HSG-04 housings, WHL-L-04 and WHL-R-04 assemblies, and MCTRL-8A.

## Tools, procedure, and warnings

Use a 2–20 N·m torque wrench, medium-strength synthetic threadlocker, and 0.20 mm alignment gauge. Install each motor in its housing, torque motor mounts to 6 N·m, fit 12:1 gearboxes, and torque axle nuts to 18 N·m. Route phases away from encoder leads.

Never install MTR-24-290 under Rev C authorization: it mechanically fits but exceeds the DRV-8A/MCTRL-8A current design point.

## Validation

Wheel runout under 0.6 mm, no-load current below 1.2 A per motor at 24 V, encoder direction correct, and left/right identifiers match firmware.
