# Rover-alpha revision change log

## Rev A — 2026-05-12 — EVT

Initial CHS-240-A1, MCB-A2, PDB-24V-A1, MTR-24-180, BAT-24-10, and CM-1080-R1 configuration.

## Rev B — 2026-06-04 — EVT2

Upgraded to MTR-24-220 and introduced a two-circuit J12 power disconnect using HAR-J12-B. WI-114-B added service-loop routing. The Rev B harness is not compatible with Rev C MCB-C4 because it lacks the enable and current-sense circuits.

## Rev C — 2026-08-18 — DVT

J12 changed to the four-circuit Molex 43025-0400 with HAR-J12-C. MCB-C4 routes pin 3 to GPIO_17 and pin 4 to ADC3. BAT-24-12, CM-4K-R2, CAL-CAM-12-C, and CHS-240-C3 were released. WI-114 and QA-FIX-C were updated for the four-circuit connector.
